Hello World and welcome to my portfolio page!

I'm Dino, the architect and engineer behind all the projects on this page. Throughout this site, I will share my experiences and thoughts regarding my adventures in the programming universe, which has definitely been interesting so far. So let's start with some background!

## Where It Started

My official programming journey started in February 2025, when I enrolled in an AP degree in Computer Science at what was formerly known as the Copenhagen Business Academy.

Back then, I wrote my first line of code in Processing, the Java-inspired open-source programming language developed in 2001 by Ben Fry and Casey Reas. Processing is specifically designed for artists, designer, and you guessed it! Me!

## Before The Code

That being said, I've never been far from a computer. Ever since I gained consciousness, I've had an interest in any type of electronic device with a screen attached to it. I was born in 1998, so needless to say, the screens I first had my eyes glued to were usually on the bulkier side compared to the paper-thin screens we see nowadays.

My first phone was a classic Nokia 3310 (the absolute pinnacle of indestructible technology). It was soon upgraded to a white and red Nokia 5300. From there came a Sony Ericsson Xperia X10 mini, followed by an iPhone 4, which has kept me loyal to Apple ever since.

## The Question

So why am I telling you this?

Well, I hadn't, until recently, stopped once and thought: "How does my phone work?" That curiosity soon developed into: "How does any technical program work?" And now here we are.
